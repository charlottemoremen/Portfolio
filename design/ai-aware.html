AI Aware: A Parental Monitoring App for Children’s AI Use

Mobile app concept · team project

Designed to address the United Nations’ third sustainability goal — ensuring healthy lives and wellbeing for all ages — AI Aware is a parental control app aimed at preventing children and teenagers from seeking medical advice from AI chatbots. Parents can identify specific topics that they want to be notified about if their child asks AI chatbots about them, such as “medication” or “stomachache cures,” and can set the intensity of the response per trigger word: a low setting for “cold” simply notifies the parent, while a high setting for a word like “steroids” blocks the AI engine until the parent re-enables it. Parents can also read AI parenting guidance and news through a dedicated resources page. This app aims to maintain digital safety for the most vulnerable members of society and prevent healthcare crises caused by medical misinformation generated by AI engines.
