Pandai: Gamified Learning

High-fidelity prototype · CS124, User Interfaces and Experiences

For this final project, the class was challenged to design an interface for a startup whose mission inspired us, without referencing its existing product. My team chose Pandai, an EdTech company that motivates students to practice academic skills through gamification. We built a high-fidelity prototype with a home, profile, and practice page, plus an interactive “animal park” where students earn tokens for daily practice to collect and display animals.
