Generational Differences in Understandings of Privacy Terminology

Charlotte Moremen, Jordan Hoogsteden, & Eleanor Birrell

Paper · Privacy Enhancing Technologies Symposium (PETs), 2024

Read the paper · Watch the talk (PETS 2024, Bristol)

In this study, we investigated how different generational groups' understand of the terminology used in privacy policies by surveying and conducting focus group sessions with 3 age cohorts: 5th graders ages 10-11, college students ages 18-23, and retirees ages 73-92. We found that all generational groups struggled to define 8 common privacy terms, despite all participants reporting being Internet users and regularly signing privacy policies, and themes of confusion and frustration were consistent across generations. In particular, young adults described a feeling of disempowerment and an inability to change the current privacy ecosystem, while older adults expressed a feeling of being left behind as society increasingly shifts online.
