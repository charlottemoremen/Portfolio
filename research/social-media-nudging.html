Social Media Usage Management: The Effectiveness of Nudging Strategies

Charlotte Moremen, Caitlin Cary Burke, & Eleanor Birrell

In progress

We are investigating the effectiveness of various nudging strategies to reduce overconsumption of social media via a third-party app I developed that tracks usage time and applies one of or both nudging methods: a gray overlay and a memory tile puzzle that must be solved before usage can continue. In an intial 2-week pilot study, we received positive feedback from users who noted that both the gray overlay and thep puzzle deterred them from excessive consumption. We look forward to expanding to a full study and providing empirical evidence that social media companies should have a fiduciary duty to their users to limit their screen time. Given the wealth of evidence that excessive social media use is directly correlated with negative mental health outcomes, social media companies could potentially be legally to obligated to help their users regulate their app usage.
