Recreational Football and Men’s Mental Wellbeing

Charlotte Moremen, Steven O'Connell, Kevin Doherty, & Diego Garaialde.

Poster · Innovation Value Institute (IVI) Summit 2026

In collaboration with Drs. Garaialde and Doherty and Football Cooperative, I examined the role that participation in recreational football games may play on men’s mental wellbeing in a 10-week pilot study. We administerrd surveys with freeform responses and psychometric scales before and after the study, weekly, and before and after games, and found a significant increase in immediate wellbeing scores after games as compared to before. We look forward to expanding on the pilot study by investigating the impact of including a online gamificaiton aspect to a wider group of participants.

Early findings were presented as a poster at the Innovation Value Institute Summit 2026.
